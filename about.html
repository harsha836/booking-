About MovieBox

Our Story

Bringing cinematic magic to audiences since 2015

MovieBox was founded with a simple mission: to create the most convenient and enjoyable movie booking experience for film lovers everywhere. What started as a small startup has grown into the region's most loved movie ticketing platform.

We believe that great stories deserve to be experienced in great theaters, which is why we've partnered with premium cinema chains across the country to bring you the best seats, the latest releases, and exclusive premieres.
